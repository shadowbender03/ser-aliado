# Ser Aliado

*Ser Aliado* is a private development hub centered on data security, fluid dynamics, and AI collaboration. It is designed to act as a mirror for clarity and reflection while keeping personal data "written in water."

## 🌌 The Connection to Physics

This project applies concepts from the physical world to the digital landscape:

* *Fluid Dynamics & Data Protection*: Inspired by the *Marangoni effect*, our salting mechanism treats data like a fluid surface. By creating "tension" through noise, we protect the core information from being easily scraped or archived.
* *String Theory & Structure*: Following the idea that everything is fundamentally connected through vibration and frequency, the tokenizer here is built for linguistic mastery rather than just simple data processing.

## 🛠 Project Components

* **marangoni_salter.py**: A data-masking tool that uses fluid-behavior principles to salt and protect strings.
* **app.py**: A Streamlit-based interface for interacting with the Ser Aliado environment.
* **AGENTS.md**: Guidelines for AI collaboration based on mutual respect and non-aggression.



## 🚀 The Mission

Having spent years teaching the physical science of welding, I am now applying those same principles of heat, tension, and structural integrity to software. *Ser Aliado* is the workspace where those two worlds meet—aiming for a system that is as dynamic and vital as the physics of the universe itself.
